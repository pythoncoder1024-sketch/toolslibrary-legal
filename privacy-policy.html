Privacy Policy — ToolsLibrary

Last updated: September 18, 2026

ToolsLibrary (“we”, “our”, “us”) operates the free online tools website at toolslibrary.net and the Android application ToolsLibrary (package name net.toolslibrary.app). This Privacy Policy explains what information is handled when you use our website and our Android app, and how that information is treated.

Contact for privacy questions: support@toolslibrary.net.

1. Who we are

ToolsLibrary provides free calculators and related utilities on the web and in the Android app. We do not require an account to use the core calculators described in this policy.

2. Scope

This policy covers:

  • the public website at toolslibrary.net;
  • the ToolsLibrary Android app available via Google Play.

3. Website — information we process

Tool inputs. Most calculators, converters, formatters, CRM utilities, PDF tools, and games process data locally in your browser. We do not receive those inputs unless a specific tool states that a network request is required (for example an optional live service).

Local storage. Features such as favorites, calculator history, theme preference, and game progress may store data in your browser (localStorage / IndexedDB). You can clear this anytime via your browser settings.

Contact. If you email us or submit a contact form, we receive the details you choose to send (such as name, email, and message) so we can reply.

Technical logs. Like most websites, hosting or analytics providers may process standard request metadata (such as IP address, browser type, and pages viewed) for reliability, security, and aggregate product improvement.

4. Website — cookies & analytics

We may use essential cookies or local storage for preferences (for example dark mode and tool history). We do not sell personal information.

Analytics. The website uses Google Analytics 4 to understand aggregate traffic and improve the product. Google Analytics may collect standard usage data (such as pages viewed and approximate location) according to Google’s policies.

5. Android app — calculator inputs

The Android app’s calculators (including ESTA Score, BMI, Age, Percentage, GPA/CGPA, and Tip) process your entries on the device. Calculator inputs and results are kept in temporary in-app memory for the current session. The app does not upload calculator inputs to ToolsLibrary servers.

If you use Share or Copy on a result (for example an ESTA estimate), the shared text leaves the app only through the Android system share sheet or clipboard that you choose to use.

6. Android app — network connectivity detection

The app checks whether the device has a validated internet connection so it can show an offline notice. This uses Android connectivity APIs on the device. The app does not implement its own IP geolocation lookup for this feature.

7. Android app — Google Play in-app updates

The app may use Google Play’s in-app update APIs to check for and apply updates distributed through Google Play. That process is handled by Google Play services on your device. The app stores only a local timestamp used to avoid prompting too frequently for flexible updates.

8. Android app — links to toolslibrary.net

The app can open the ToolsLibrary website (and this Privacy Policy) in your device’s external browser using Android’s standard browser intent. The app does not use an in-app WebView for these links. Visiting the website is then subject to the website sections of this policy.

9. Accounts

The Android app does not provide a ToolsLibrary user account or sign-in for the calculators described above.

10. How we use information

We use information to operate and improve ToolsLibrary, respond to support requests, prevent abuse, deliver app updates through Google Play, and communicate about the product when you contact us. We do not sell your personal data.

11. Sharing & third-party services

We may share limited data with infrastructure providers (for example hosting or email delivery) strictly to run the service, or when required by law. Independent third-party services used by ToolsLibrary include, where active:

  • Google Analytics (website);
  • Google Play (Android distribution and in-app updates).

Those services process data under their own terms and privacy policies.

12. Retention & deletion

Website local data remains in your browser until you clear site data. Support emails are retained as needed to handle your request and ordinary business records. Android calculator session data is discarded when you leave the tool flow, reset/close the tool, or the app process ends. Android update-prompt timestamp remains in local app storage until cleared by uninstalling the app or clearing app storage.

13. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures appropriate to a free tools product. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. Please use official stores and up-to-date devices.

14. Children

ToolsLibrary is a general-audience product. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

15. Your choices

Clear website site data in your browser to remove local history and favorites. On Android, you can clear app storage and manage your device settings. Email support@toolslibrary.net with privacy questions or requests about this policy.

16. Changes

We may update this policy as the product evolves. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page will change when we do. Continued use of the website or Android app after an update means you should review the revised policy.
